How to read the timetable

  • Evening group A / B — the same course taught twice, pick one slot when you enrol.
  • Self-study — no live class; the practice task for the week is published instead.
  • Consultations — open room where any student may join and ask questions.
  • Open lecture — a guest talk, free to attend even without a subscription.

Joining a live class

Four steps, every single lesson

  1. Open the student portal 10 minutes before the start time.
  2. Click the room link on the card of your group.
  3. Turn the microphone on when you want to ask something — interrupting is encouraged.
  4. Download the lesson notes and start the practice task the same evening.

Attendance rules

  • Recordings appear in the portal within two hours after a class.
  • Missing more than four classes in a module means repeating it for free in the next cohort.
  • Practice tasks are accepted until Sunday 23:59 of the same week.
  • Timetable changes are announced in the student chat at least three days ahead.

Need a different slot? Ask on the contact page — if five students want the same time, we open a new group.
